## TSword PS5 0.5.4-ut1

**Frame generation now sets the game to 30 fps, for a steady 60 on the TV.**

Frame generation only gives an even picture when the game's own frames come evenly, half the TV's 60. Above that, the game can't hold its limit in heavy areas (40–55 there): frames arrive unevenly, more frames are made than the TV can show, and the picture judders.

- **Turning frame generation on now also sets the frame rate to 30, from the next start.** The quick menu lists it under "At the next start".
- At 30 the game paces itself the way it does on the Switch, which is very steady. 2x frame generation turns that into 60 on the TV.
- You can still pick another frame rate afterwards.

No new export beyond 0.5.2's. Replace the title folder's files.
