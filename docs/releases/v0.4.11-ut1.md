## TSword PS5 0.4.11-ut1

**TSword reached "The Legend of Zelda" title text with the opening's audio** on 0.4.10, then the console crashed.

**Fixed: 0.4.10's log writer wrote to the wrong file.** It wrote to descriptor 2, the usual standard error. On the console, the log is opened by `freopen`, which doesn't keep it at 2. 0.4.10's log had 18 lines; every other line went to whatever file descriptor 2 was at that moment, possibly another open file or device. That may itself have caused the crash.

- The writer now writes to `fileno(stderr)`, the log's real descriptor, looked up for every batch.
- `stdout` is joined to the log's real descriptor too.

Everything else is as in 0.4.10: the log written by its own thread, shaders kept between starts, and safe mode.

**No new PS5 export is needed.** Replace the title folder's files only.
