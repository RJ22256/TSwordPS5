## TSword PS5 0.5.8-ut1

**A test build: which graphics calls cost the frame rate.**

### What 0.5.7's logs showed
- **Drop to 30 when slow makes no difference.** With it off, the game never held itself to 30, and it still ran at 27–30 fps. Each frame really takes about 34 ms. You can leave it on.
- **On the game's main thread, per frame:**
  - about 12–14 ms in graphics calls. That's UltraTears translating the game's Switch graphics commands.
  - about 7–8 ms of the game's own work.
  - about 14 ms waiting for the game's other two threads to finish their part of the frame.
- **GPU:** about 12–14 ms, running alongside the CPU work rather than holding it up.
- So most of each frame is the port's graphics translation, not Tears of the Kingdom itself. That's the part we can speed up.

### What's new
- **An `[nvnprof]` line** with every `[frametime]` line. It shows:
  - the graphics calls' time per frame, on the main thread and on the game's other threads
  - the 12 functions that take the longest, each with its time per frame, how often it's called and how much of it is on the main thread
- **`[frametime]` also shows the main thread's yields:** time it spends checking whether its other threads are done.
- Timing every call costs a little. If this build feels slower than 0.5.7, that's why, and it'll be gone in the next one. To turn it off, add the line `TOTK_NVN_PROF=0` to `ultratears.cfg`.
