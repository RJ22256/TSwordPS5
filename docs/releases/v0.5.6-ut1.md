## TSword PS5 0.5.6-ut1

**A test build: where the frame rate goes in gameplay, and a switch to stop the game dropping to 30.**

### Why it sits at 30
TotK picks its own frame rate as it runs. It times its own work each frame, and when that takes longer than a 60 fps frame (16.7 ms), it shows each frame twice: 30 fps. It stays there until its work is quick again. So a console that could manage 40 or 50 still shows 30.

### What's new
- **Settings → Performance → Drop to 30 when slow:**
  - **On** (the default) is how the game has always done it.
  - **Off:** the game never drops to 30. It runs as fast as it can, and the game's speed stays right (it's stepped by the time frames really take).
  - It's uneven on a 60 Hz TV and smoother on a 120 Hz one.
  - It applies at the next start.
- **A gameplay line in the log:** `[frametime]`, written for every 300 frames of gameplay. The title, menus and loading are left out. It shows:
  - the frame rate and the frame times
  - the game's own work per frame, and how often it's over 16.7 ms
  - how often the game held itself to 30
  - the GPU's time per frame
  - how long the game's main thread ran, and how long it waited for a CPU core
