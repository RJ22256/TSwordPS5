**TSword PS5 - milestone 4, fix 12: WSword's texture checks on the console.**

TSword now uses the texture check WSword (Breath of the Wild) uses on the PS5, in place of v0.3.12's.

- **What it does:** the PS5 can't tell TSword which memory the game wrote to, so TSword checks each texture's own data instead. As in WSword: a texture is fingerprinted in full when it's first decoded; after that it's checked at most once a frame, every 8th frame for a texture that has never changed (a quick check of 2,048 spots, and a full fingerprint when those differ, or every 120 frames). A texture the game has changed once is checked in full every frame. A different fingerprint means it's decoded again.
- **Why:** v0.3.12 fingerprinted every byte of every texture on screen for every frame. That is exact, but in the open world it's hundreds of MB a frame. WSword's schedule reads a small fraction of that, and it's what BotW already runs with on your PS5.
- `TOTK_TEX_FULLHASH=1` in `totk.ini` goes back to checking every texture in full every frame.

Copy only the new `PPSA99778` title folder. The game code and shaders on your USB drive (`TSword` folder) stay as they are.
